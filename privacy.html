Privacy policy.

How Jarvis Ultimate accesses and uses information when you connect an authorized YouTube channel.

Effective date: November 13, 2026 · Application: Jarvis Ultimate desktop software

1. Who this policy covers

Jarvis Ultimate is privately operated Windows desktop software for content research, short-form video editing, and management of a YouTube channel that its operator has explicitly connected. This policy covers data handled by the current Jarvis Ultimate content application, including its Google/YouTube connection features. The public website itself does not create accounts.

2. Google and YouTube data accessed

When you authorize the application through Google's permission screen, Jarvis requests the YouTube read-only and YouTube upload permissions. Depending on the action you initiate, the application may access your connected channel's name, identification information, published video metadata, and the information needed to upload videos to that channel. For research, Jarvis also retrieves public YouTube video and channel metadata, such as titles, view counts, and upload dates.

Jarvis does not use the YouTube API to retrieve or download videos for republishing. Source video files for clipping must be supplied separately by the rights holder or authorized operator.

3. How data is used

Google user data is used to identify the channel you authorized, authenticate requests, research video performance, and upload videos when you direct the application to do so. Jarvis does not sell Google user data, use it for targeted advertising, or share it with data brokers. It does not use Google user data to train generalized AI models.

Limited Use: Jarvis Ultimate's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

4. How and where information is stored

The Windows desktop application saves the Google OAuth client configuration and Google authorization tokens in encrypted local storage protected by Windows Data Protection API (DPAPI), bound to the local Windows account. It also stores user-selected settings, production history, transcript-based processing outputs, exported videos, and trend-research records under the operator's local application-data folder. These are not stored on this public website.

For the current release, the default local folder is %LOCALAPPDATA%\JarvisUltimateContent. Authorization data stays until the operator removes it, changes Windows accounts, uninstalls and clears local data, or revokes access. Data saved to YouTube is subject to YouTube's own retention policies.

5. AI processing and third parties

Where configured, Jarvis uses a local LM Studio server and locally installed transcription and video tools to analyze supplied footage. Its standard local endpoint is on the operator's computer; no cloud AI vendor is required. If the operator deliberately changes the AI endpoint to another service, information sent to that service is governed by that provider's practices; this should be reviewed before doing so.

YouTube authentication and publishing requests are sent to Google-operated services. Google processes data under its own terms and privacy policies. The public Jarvis website is static, has no advertising pixels, has no analytics scripts, and does not set its own tracking cookies.

6. Optional email notifications

If enabled, Jarvis sends upload confirmations through Gmail SMTP using an operator-provided Gmail address and a separately configured Gmail app password. The app password is encrypted locally using Windows DPAPI. Messages may contain a video title, upload status, and link. Messages are delivered through Google's Gmail service.

7. Your choices and control

  • You choose which Google account and channel to authorize.
  • You control upload visibility and whether automatic upload is enabled in the desktop app.
  • You can disconnect by revoking Jarvis Ultimate's permissions from your Google Account permissions.
  • You can delete saved local Jarvis data under the application-data folder after closing the app. Back up any videos you wish to retain first.
  • For help or requests regarding the application, contact the address below.

8. Updates to this policy

This page will be updated if Jarvis's actual data uses, connected services, or retention practices change. Substantial changes may require renewed permission through Google.
